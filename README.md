# 🚚 Supply Chain Logistics & Inventory Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This Tableau project provides an end-to-end analysis of Supply Chain Logistics and Inventory Management operations. The dashboard helps monitor supplier performance, inventory distribution, delivery efficiency, warehouse delays, transportation costs, and demand patterns to support better operational decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor logistics performance across regions
- Evaluate supplier efficiency and reliability
- Analyze inventory levels across product categories
- Track monthly delivery trends
- Identify warehouse delay patterns
- Understand inventory versus demand relationships
- Improve forecasting and inventory planning

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Result | Business Impact |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| 📦 Total Orders | 1,000 | Operational Volume |
| 🚚 On-Time Delivery Rate | 50.60% | Delivery Performance |
| 🔄 Inventory Turnover | 72.18% | Inventory Efficiency |
| ⚠️ Stockout Rate | 17.50% | Product Availability |
| 💰 Transportation Cost | ₹2,77,13,739 | Logistics Spending |
| 🎯 Forecast Accuracy | 99.76% | Demand Planning Accuracy |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 📊 Supplier Performance Analysis
Evaluates supplier efficiency using performance scores and delivery metrics.

### 📦 Inventory by Product Category
Compares inventory levels across multiple product categories.

### 📈 Monthly Delivery Trend
Tracks monthly On-Time and Delayed deliveries to identify logistics trends.

### 🏭 Warehouse Delay Analysis
Highlights warehouse-level delivery delays across different regions.

### 🔍 Inventory vs Demand Analysis
Examines the relationship between inventory levels and actual customer demand.

### 🌍 Region-wise Logistics Performance
Measures logistics performance across operational regions.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

✅ Gati achieved the highest supplier performance score.

✅ Food & Beverage maintained the highest inventory levels among all categories.

✅ Forecast Accuracy reached 99.76%, indicating highly effective demand planning.

✅ Central Region recorded the highest number of On-Time Deliveries.

✅ Strong positive correlation observed between Inventory Levels and Actual Demand.

✅ Stockout Rate of 17.50% indicates opportunities for inventory optimization.
